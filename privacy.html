Privacy Policy

Draft for owner review. This describes how the site currently behaves. Have it reviewed before relying on it.

What we collect

If you use the estimate form, nothing is sent to us by the website. The form writes your request as a text message on your own device, and nothing is sent until you press Send in your messaging app. If you choose to send it, we receive what you put in the message: for example your name, phone number, address or town, the service you want and your project details. We use it only to reply to your request and to do the work you ask about.

Cookies and tracking

This site does not set its own cookies and does not run analytics or advertising trackers. Fonts and images are served from this site, not from a third party.

Third parties

Links to Google reviews take you to Google, which has its own privacy policy. If you open the Copy or Text options, your device's apps handle the message under their own policies.

Sharing

We don't sell your information.

Contact

Questions or requests to delete your information: call 610-357-1098.
