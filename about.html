Who we are

Wolf's Landscaping Services is an owner-operated landscaping company serving Royersford and the surrounding Chester County area. When you call or send a request, you deal with the people who will actually be working on your property.

We look after lawns and yards week to week, and we build the bigger things too: beds and plantings, walkways and steps, retaining walls, drainage and grading, new lawns, seasonal cleanups, tree work and snow removal.
