Landscape Design & Installation

Beds, borders and plantings that fit how you live.

Request a Free Estimate

Some yards just need a fresh start. Overgrown beds, tired ground cover, a corner nobody knows what to do with. We help you decide what should stay, what should go, and what would make the space work better.

Then we build it. New beds, mulch, plantings, relocating shrubs, finishing touches. We keep the job site neat and the plan simple, so you see the result you pictured.

What's included

  • Garden and landscape design
  • New planting beds and borders
  • Mulching and weed control
  • Removing old ground cover and overgrowth
  • Relocating and planting shrubs, flowers and trees
  • Garden decorating and finishing touches

How we work

We listen first.

Tell us what you want and what bugs you about the space. We design around that, not a template.

Work that stays on budget.

We agree on the plan and the price before we start, then stick to it.

Clean and tidy.

We clear our mess as we go and leave the area ready to enjoy.

Common questions

Do you do small projects?

Yes. A single bed or a corner redo is just as welcome as a bigger job.

Can you work with plants I already have?

Often, yes. We're happy to save and relocate what's worth keeping.

When is the best time to plant?

Spring and fall are usually best. We'll talk timing when we see your yard.

Can you also build walls or walkways for the design?

Yes. See hardscaping and retaining walls.
