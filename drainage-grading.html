Drainage & Grading

Move the water. Protect the yard.

Request a Free Estimate

Standing water, soggy patches and soil washing toward the house are signs your yard isn't draining the way it should. The good news is that most of these problems can be fixed.

We look at how water actually moves across your property, then regrade or reshape the ground to send it where it should go.

What's included

  • Yard drainage solutions
  • Yard grading
  • Grading and resloping
  • Reshaping the ground away from the house
  • Prep work for lawns, beds and hardscape

Common questions

How do I fix yard drainage problems?

Start by finding where the water collects and where it comes from. Often the fix is regrading so water moves away from the house, sometimes with a wall or other drainage work. We'll look at your yard and tell you what makes sense. Request a free estimate.

Why is my yard always wet?

Usually it's low spots, poor slope or compacted soil. We'll check on site.

Can you grade and then reseed?

Yes. We can take it from rough ground to finished lawn. See aeration, sod and seeding.

Do you work on small problem areas?

Yes. Many jobs are a single low spot or one side of the house.
