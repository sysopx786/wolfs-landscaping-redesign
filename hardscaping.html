Hardscaping

Stone, steps and walkways built to last.

Request a Free Estimate

Hardscaping is the part of your yard that has to hold up. People walk on it, cars drive over it, and Pennsylvania weather tests it every year. So we focus on a solid base and careful finish work, because that's what you can't see and what matters most.

Whether you want a better path to the front door, safer steps, or stone that gives a bed some character, we'll talk it through and give you a straight answer on what makes sense.

What's included

  • Driveway and walkway paving
  • Outdoor step construction
  • Stone and rock landscaping
  • Walkway and driveway landscaping
  • General hardscape repair and upgrades

How we work

Built from the ground up.

A good surface starts with good prep. We don't skip the steps you can't see.

Options for your budget.

There's more than one way to build a walkway. We'll show you the choices and what they cost.

Fits your home.

Straight or curved, formal or relaxed, we match the style of your property.

Common questions

How much does a paver walkway cost?

It depends on the length, width, materials and how much prep your site needs. We look at the space and give you a clear price before any work starts. Request a free estimate.

Can you add steps to a sloped yard?

Yes. Steps and retaining walls often go together on a slope. See our retaining walls page.

How long does a walkway take?

It depends on size and conditions. We'll give you a realistic timeline with your estimate.

Can you match the stone I already have?

We'll do our best. Send us a photo and we'll talk it through.
